# Execute a complete search using only keyboard navigation

## Step 1
Navigate to https://airbnb.co.in.

## Step 2
Press the **Tab** key repeatedly until the **Where** input field is focused.

## Step 3
Type "Goa" into the focused field.

## Step 4
Press **Tab** to move focus to the **Check-in** field and press **Enter** to open the calendar.

## Step 5
Use the arrow keys to select a valid check-in date and press **Enter**.

## Step 6
Use the arrow keys to select a valid check-out date and press **Enter**.

## Step 7
Press **Tab** to move focus to the **Who** field and press **Enter**.

## Step 8
Press **Tab** to focus the 'add' button for Adults and press **Enter**.

## Step 9
Press **Tab** until the **Search** button is focused.

## Step 10
Press the **Enter** key.

## Step 11
Verify the search parameters are preserved on the results page.
