# Refine partial search results by applying a filter

## Step 1
Navigate to https://www.airbnb.co.in.

## Step 2
In the **Where** field, enter "Goa" and in the **Who** field, add "2" guests.

## Step 3
Click the **Search** button.

## Step 4
Note the initial number of search results displayed (e.g., "Over 1,000 homes").

## Step 5
Click the **Filters** button on the results page.

## Step 6
Select a common amenity filter, such as "Wifi".

## Step 7
Click the **Show homes** button to apply the filter.

## Step 8
Verify the search results have updated.

## Step 9
Verify the **Filters** button indicates that a filter is active.
