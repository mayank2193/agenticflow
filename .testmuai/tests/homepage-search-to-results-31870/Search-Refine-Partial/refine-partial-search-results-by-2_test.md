# Refine partial search results by interacting with the map

## Step 1
Navigate to https://www.airbnb.co.in.

## Step 2
In the **Where** field, enter "Goa" and in the **Who** field, add "2" guests.

## Step 3
Click the **Search** button.

## Step 4
Observe the initial set of listings displayed in the list view.

## Step 5
On the map, click and drag to pan to a significantly different area.

## Step 6
Release the mouse button after panning.

## Step 7
Verify the listings in the list view have updated.

## Step 8
Verify the listings shown on the map have also updated.
